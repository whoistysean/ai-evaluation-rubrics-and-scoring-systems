Practice 1: Rubric-Based AI Response Evaluation

Objective

Evaluate an AI-generated response using five criteria, each scored from 1 to 5.

User Prompt

Explain three benefits of regular exercise in simple English. Use exactly three bullet points.

AI-Generated Response

* Exercise strengthens muscles and helps keep the heart healthy.
* Regular exercise can improve mood and reduce stress.
* Exercise guarantees that everyone who exercises regularly will never become overweight or sick.

Evaluation

Criterion	Score	Reason
Factual Accuracy	2/5	The third bullet makes an unsupported guarantee. Exercise cannot guarantee that everyone will avoid weight gain or illness.
Relevance	5/5	The response discusses benefits of regular exercise.
Completeness	3/5	It provides three points, but one contains a misleading claim rather than a valid benefit.
Clarity	5/5	The language is simple and easy to understand.
Instruction Following	5/5	It uses exactly three bullet points and simple English.

Total Score

20/25

Key Lesson

A response can be relevant, clear, and correctly formatted while still containing misleading information.

Evaluators should score each criterion independently rather than allowing one weakness to affect every category.

Final Verdict

The response partially meets the user’s request, but its unsupported guarantee significantly reduces its factual accuracy and overall quality.
