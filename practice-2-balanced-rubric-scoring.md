Practice 2: Balanced Rubric Scoring

Objective

Practise applying all five evaluation criteria independently when an AI response performs well.

User Prompt

Explain photosynthesis to a 10-year-old in exactly three simple bullet points.

AI-Generated Response

* Plants use sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide to make their own food.
* Chlorophyll, the green pigment in plants, helps capture energy from sunlight.
* During photosynthesis, plants release oxygen into the air.

Evaluation

Criterion	Score	Reason
Factual Accuracy	5/5	The explanation accurately describes the basic process of photosynthesis.
Relevance	5/5	Every bullet directly explains photosynthesis.
Completeness	5/5	It includes the essential ingredients, role of chlorophyll, and oxygen release.
Clarity	5/5	The language is simple and understandable for a 10-year-old.
Instruction Following	5/5	It uses exactly three simple bullet points as requested.

Total Score

25/25

Key Lesson

A strong response should satisfy each evaluation criterion independently. A high score is justified when the response is accurate, relevant, complete enough for the task, clear, and follows all instructions.

Final Verdict

The response fully satisfies the user’s request and earns an excellent score of 25/25.
